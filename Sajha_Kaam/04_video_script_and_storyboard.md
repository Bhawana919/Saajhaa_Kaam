## Performance and editorial notes

- **Girl 1** establishes human stakes with nuance, not a promise to stop all migration.
- **Girl 2** demonstrates exact product steps and explicitly distinguishes simulated payment from settlement.
- **Girl 3** explains legal feasibility and closes with testable targets.
- Show three real people with consent. Use clean subtitles and a clear source footnote on the permit claim. Do not depict the permit count as a number of unique emigrants.
- Product visual project: `video/video.html` and `video/video.js`. It is animated at 60 FPS when played and time-synced to the shot list; press **Record WebM** to save its visual track. Record the three voices separately and combine in an editor. For a combined MP4, capture voices and the exported WebM, then use any editor or ffmpeg; there is no spoken audio embedded in this ZIP.
