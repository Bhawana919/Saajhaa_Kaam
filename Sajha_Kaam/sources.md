# Research notes · accessed 26 September 2026

These are source links for the contest narrative and launch guardrails. Architecture and scoring advice elsewhere in the package are **design proposals**, not claims made by the sources.

1. [ILO, Nepal strengthens capacities to better negotiate labour migration agreements](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/nepal-strengthens-capacities-better-negotiate-labour-migration-agreements), 2025: says **over 740,000 labour permits** were issued in FY 2023/24. A permit count is not a count of distinct people leaving; renewals may be included. Do not repeat the contest brief's “200,000 people emigrate each year” as a verified current count.
2. [World Bank, Unlocking Nepal’s Growth Potential](https://www.worldbank.org/en/country/nepal/publication/unlocking-nepal-s-growth-potential), 2025: limited domestic quality jobs, over 7% abroad by 2021, remittances approximately a quarter of GDP in 2023. Supports urgency, not a claim that every migrant could work remotely.
3. [World Bank, Connecting Nepal with the Global Digital Economy](https://www.worldbank.org/en/events/2023/04/12/connecting-nepal-with-the-global-digital-economy), 2023: notes growing IT services and Nepal's digital development ambitions. Does not quantify addressable freelance market.
4. [Nepal Department of Industry, Foreign Investment](https://www.doind.gov.np/foreign-investment) and [One Stop Service Center](https://doind.gov.np/ossc): foreign investment is administered under national legislation and approvals. A municipality or app cannot confer an alternative investment regime.
5. [Nepal Rastra Bank, Foreign Exchange Management Department](https://www.nrb.org.np/departments/fxm/) and [integrated foreign-exchange circular archive](https://www.nrb.org.np/category/fxm-circulars/%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A6%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%80-%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%AF-%E0%A4%8F%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%80%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%83%E0%A4%A4/?department=fxm): NRB regulates foreign exchange and licenses institutions. Product must use an authorized settlement path; do not assert an exact provider, fee, FX rate or tax withholding before agreement and review.
6. [Nepal Inland Revenue Department, tax-law and procedure archive](https://ird.gov.np/tax-laws/category/procedure): tax law and procedures can change. The app should generate records, request PAN when required, and refer users to current professional advice; avoid hard-coded blanket rates.
7. [ILO, Convention No. 193 on decent work in the platform economy](https://www.ilo.org/resource/other/c193-decent-work-platform-economy-convention-2026) and [ILO Nepal summary](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/ilo-adopts-first-global-convention-decent-work-platform-economy): useful design principles for clear engagement terms, payment information, dispute process, privacy and correct worker classification. Adoption is **not** a claim that Nepal has ratified or implemented it.

Last checked: 26 September 2026. Verify law and partner capability again before any real launch.
