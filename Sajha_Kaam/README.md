# Sajha Kaam · साझाकाम

**A practical bridge from Nepali skill to global paid work**  
Hackathon concept package · 26 September 2026

## Start here

1. Read `01_strategy_and_pitch.md` for the policy critique, chosen idea, prototype scope and hackathon pitch.
2. Open `prototype/index.html` in a modern browser to click through the mobile experience. All demo people, jobs, amounts and payment states are fictional.
3. Open `diagrams/` for PNG and editable SVG architecture and screen drawings.
4. Use `02_product_and_architecture.md` for detailed UX, APIs, data model, trust rules, security and data flow.
5. Use `03_ai_build_prompt.md` as the implementation prompt for a coding agent.
6. Read `04_video_script_and_storyboard.md`. Open `video/video.html` for a 120-second JavaScript animation, use the controls to preview, and use its **Record WebM** button in a compatible desktop browser to export. The project does not contain filmed speakers or cloned voices.

### Design language

Off-white paper `#F6F4EE`, ink `#252A25`, brick `#A64C32`, sage `#6B7561`, pale clay `#EAE5DB`, borders `#D5D1C7`. Flat fills only. No gradients, glass, neon, confetti, mascots or AI stock illustrations. System and local Nepali capable fonts; SVG diagrams use system fallbacks. Mobile layout is 390 × 844 reference size.

### Important distinctions

- A **Sajha Work Passport** is a portable, consented portfolio and transaction record, **not** a government ID, work permit, tax registration or foreign legal identity.
- A signed milestone is a product record of agreement. Whether a contract is enforceable depends on actual law and parties' circumstances. The demo uses fictional and simulated payment states; Sajha Kaam does **not** hold customer money or claim escrow capability.
- A bank or licensed payment partner handles real international settlement after diligence and a launch arrangement. Tax guidance is a checklist with local professional review, never a universal automatic rate.
- Municipality partnerships may help with training and access, but cannot create a new jurisdiction or waive national law.

## Demo walkthrough (90 seconds)

Choose **Find work** → view a narrow, clearly priced project → **Apply with proof** → see a sample application. Switch role to **Hire talent**, inspect proof-of-work, create a milestone. Switch back to **My work** to see delivery and payment status. Tap **Help** to see a dispute pathway and a human escalation. Back on the home screen, notice the three-step learning path: complete a practical sample, get a client review, then unlock a larger assignment. No ranking by popularity.

## Submission positioning

**One sentence:** “Sajha Kaam turns a first paid cross-border project into repeatable, verifiable trust.”  
**Ask:** pilot with one university or municipality skills hub, one licensed payment partner, 20 vetted workers, and five diaspora-led companies. These are proposed pilot targets, not current partners or measured impact.

## Demo vs deployment

The prototype is local, static and deliberately uses sample transactions. For a deployed pilot, implement the API, data policies and partner integrations in `02_product_and_architecture.md`; obtain legal, tax, privacy and payment reviews before transacting. Do not represent the demo badge, payment state or sample contracts as certification by any government, bank or partner.
